How To Play

Wordle : Guess the hidden word in six tries or less. Type letters and press Enter to submit a guess. After each guess, the tiles change color:

E G W

  • Green — right letter, right spot.
  • Pink — right letter, wrong spot.
  • Gray — the letter isn't in the word.

Word length changes daily — sometimes four letters, sometimes seven.

Squaredle — Find every hidden word in the 4x4 grid for that day's theme. Drag (or swipe on mobile) through connected letters — up, down, sideways, or diagonally — to trace a word, or just type it and press Enter. Once every word running through a square has been found, that square fades out.

Use the archive (the calendar icon) to play any puzzle from a day that's already passed.
